Our story

Built by retreat-goers, for retreat-goers.

We started bookretreat.yoga because finding the right yoga retreat was harder than it should be.

The biggest sites had thousands of listings — overwhelming, generic, hard to trust. The smallest hosts had beautiful retreats but no easy way to be found. And the people in between, the ones quietly seeking a week of stillness, were stuck reading endless reviews trying to figure out which retreats were actually held with care.

So we built something simpler. A small, hand-picked collection. Every host personally spoken to. Every retreat reviewed for the details that matter — the food, the teachers, the silence, the small kindnesses. No paid placements. No fake reviews.

If you find one that calls to you, tell us. We'll make sure you get a real answer from a real person who's been there.
