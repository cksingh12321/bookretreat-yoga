Choosing a teacher training

📜

200-hour

The standard entry-level qualification. Usually 21–28 days. Yoga Alliance RYS-200 lets you teach almost anywhere.

🪷

300-hour

Advanced — requires RYT-200. Refines your teaching, opens RYT-500 status, and lets you lead specialty workshops.

🕉

Immersion

Traditional, lineage-based study. No Yoga Alliance certificate, but deep practice in the way the tradition is actually taught.
